Protein per 100 calories, ten foods ranked

Grams per 100 g tell you how dense a food is. Grams per 100 kcal tell you how much protein you get for the energy you eat.

Source USDA FoodData Central, SR LegacyRecords 10Formula protein g ÷ kcal × 100Retrieved 2026-11-16

The ranking

  • Greek yogurt, plain nonfat17.3 g
  • Spinach, raw12.4 g
  • Atlantic salmon, farmed9.8 g
  • Whole eggs8.8 g
  • Broccoli, raw8.3 g
  • Lentils, cooked7.8 g
  • Chickpeas, cooked5.4 g
  • Almonds3.7 g
  • Rolled oats3.5 g
  • Brown rice, cooked2.2 g

Greek yogurt, plain nonfat leads with 17.3 g of protein per 100 kcal. Brown rice, cooked sits last at 2.2 g, even though it is still a useful food: their energy comes mostly from fat.

Why both numbers matter

Per 100 g suits comparing foods you eat by weight. Per 100 kcal suits comparing foods when you care about energy, because it removes the effect of water and fat. Spinach is a good example: it has little protein per 100 g, but because it has so few calories, its protein per 100 kcal is high.

Check a portion

Nobody eats exactly 100 g. Use the portion calculator to scale any food to the weight on your plate, and read how to read per-100 g values before comparing cooked and dry foods.
