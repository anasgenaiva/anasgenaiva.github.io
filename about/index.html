About Plate Notes

Plate Notes is a small, non-commercial reference. It shows what 100 grams of ten everyday foods holds and builds a few simple recipes from them.

Where the numbers come from

All nutrient values come from USDA FoodData Central, SR Legacy dataset, which is in the public domain. Each food page shows its record number and the date the data was retrieved (2026-11-16). Nothing is estimated except the per-serving recipe totals and the protein-per-100 kcal figures, which are calculated from those records.

The SEO lab

The site also works as a practice ground for Google Search Console. The SEO lab holds pages that are broken on purpose. Each says so at the top and explains which Search Console status it should produce.

What this site is not

It is not a clinic, a dietitian or a business, and it gives no medical or dietary advice. It has no ads, no affiliate links and no reviews.

Plate Notes: per-100 g food facts from USDA FoodData Central, and a lab for learning Google Search Console. General information, not medical advice.
