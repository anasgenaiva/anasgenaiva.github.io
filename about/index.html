    About Plate Notes

    Plate Notes is a small, non-commercial nutrition reference. It shows what 100 grams of 40 everyday foods holds, ranks them nutrient by nutrient, compares them in pairs, and builds a few simple recipes from them.

    Where the numbers come from

    All nutrient values come from USDA FoodData Central, SR Legacy dataset, which is in the public domain. Each food page names its record number. Nothing is estimated except recipe totals, energy shares and protein per 100 kcal, which are calculated from those records.

    The SEO lab

    The site doubles as a practice ground for Google Search Console. The SEO lab holds pages that are broken on purpose; each says so at the top and explains which Search Console status it should produce.

    What this site is not

    It is not a clinic, a dietitian or a business, and it gives no medical or dietary advice. It has no ads, no affiliate links and no reviews.
