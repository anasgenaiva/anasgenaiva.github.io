    Top lists

    All 40 foods ranked by one nutrient at a time.

    Foods highest in protein per 100 g

    Protein helps build and repair tissue. This list ranks every food on the site by grams of protein in 100 g.

    Top 3: Pumpkin seeds, Peanuts, Sardines

    Foods highest in fibre per 100 g

    Fibre is the part of plant foods the body cannot digest. Here every food on the site is ranked by grams of fibre in 100 g.

    Top 3: Flaxseed, Almonds, Rolled oats

    Foods highest in iron per 100 g

    Iron is part of haemoglobin, which carries oxygen in the blood. Every food on the site, ranked by milligrams of iron in 100 g.

    Top 3: Pumpkin seeds, Flaxseed, Peanuts

    Foods highest in calcium per 100 g

    Calcium is the main mineral in bones and teeth. Every food on the site, ranked by milligrams of calcium in 100 g.

    Top 3: Cheddar cheese, Sardines, Almonds

    Foods highest in potassium per 100 g

    Potassium helps nerves and muscles work. Every food on the site, ranked by milligrams of potassium in 100 g.

    Top 3: Flaxseed, Pumpkin seeds, Almonds

    Lowest-calorie foods per 100 g

    Energy density is how many calories a food packs into its weight. Every food on the site, from lowest to highest kcal per 100 g.

    Top 3: Tomatoes, Spinach, Broccoli

    Most protein per 100 calories

    Protein per 100 kcal shows how much protein you get for the energy you eat. It removes the effect of water and fat.

    Top 3: Light tuna, Atlantic cod, Greek yogurt
