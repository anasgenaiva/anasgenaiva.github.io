Baked salmon with brown rice and broccoli

Oven-baked salmon fillets with brown rice and steamed broccoli, ready in 20 minutes.

Prep 5 minCook 15 minPer serving 531 kcal · 37.6 g protein · 5 g fibreData USDA FDC, tracked ingredients only

Ingredients

  • 300 g atlantic salmon, farmed
  • 300 g brown rice, cooked
  • 200 g broccoli, raw
  • Salt and black pepper
  • Lemon wedges to serve

Method

  1. Heat the oven to 200 °C (400 °F).
  2. Season the salmon and bake on a lined tray for 12 to 15 minutes, until it flakes easily.
  3. Steam the broccoli for 4 to 5 minutes and warm the rice.
  4. Serve the salmon on the rice with the broccoli and lemon.
