    Lentil and spinach soup

    Cooked lentils simmered with cumin, thickened by mashing, and finished with fresh spinach.

    Energy183kcal per serving
    Protein14.6 gper serving
    Prep10 mincook 15 min
    Fibre12.7 gper serving

    Ingredients

    • 600 g lentils, cooked
    • 150 g spinach, raw
    • 800 ml water
    • 1 tsp ground cumin
    • Juice of half a lemon
    • Salt to taste

    Method

    1. Warm the cooked lentils with the water and cumin in a large pan.
    2. Simmer for 10 minutes, then mash about a third of the lentils to thicken the soup.
    3. Stir in the spinach until it wilts, about 2 minutes.
    4. Season with lemon juice and salt.
    Nutrition counts the linked ingredients at the weights listed, from their USDA records. Water, spices, lemon and added salt are not counted; added salt raises sodium.
