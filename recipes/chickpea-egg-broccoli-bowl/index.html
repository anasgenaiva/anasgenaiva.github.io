    Chickpea, egg and broccoli rice bowl

    Brown rice and chickpeas topped with boiled eggs and steamed broccoli.

    Energy487kcal per serving
    Protein23.8 gper serving
    Prep10 mincook 12 min
    Fibre14.1 gper serving

    Ingredients

    • 300 g brown rice, cooked
    • 240 g chickpeas, cooked
    • 100 g whole eggs
    • 200 g broccoli, raw
    • Salt and black pepper
    • Lemon wedges to serve

    Method

    1. Boil the eggs for 9 minutes, cool them in cold water and peel.
    2. Steam the broccoli for 4 to 5 minutes until just tender.
    3. Warm the rice and chickpeas.
    4. Divide between two bowls, top with halved eggs and broccoli, and season.
    Nutrition counts the linked ingredients at the weights listed, from their USDA records. Water, spices, lemon and added salt are not counted; added salt raises sodium.
