    Overnight oats with Greek yogurt and almonds

    A no-cook breakfast: oats softened overnight in Greek yogurt, finished with chopped almonds.

    Energy365kcal per serving
    Protein25 gper serving
    Prep5 min8 hours in the fridge
    Fibre6.9 gper serving

    Ingredients

    • 50 g rolled oats
    • 150 g greek yogurt, plain nonfat
    • 15 g almonds
    • 60 ml water or milk (water assumed in the nutrition)
    • A pinch of cinnamon (optional)

    Method

    1. Stir the oats, yogurt and water together in a jar.
    2. Cover and leave in the fridge for at least 8 hours.
    3. Chop the almonds and scatter them on top just before eating.
    Nutrition counts the linked ingredients at the weights listed, from their USDA records. Water, spices, lemon and added salt are not counted; added salt raises sodium.
