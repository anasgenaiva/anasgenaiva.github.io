Lentils, cooked: nutrition per 100 g

116 kcal, 9 g protein and 7.9 g fibre in every 100 grams.

Source USDA FoodData CentralRecord FDC 172421NDB 16070Dataset SR LegacyPublished 2019-04-01Basis per 100 g edible portionRetrieved 2026-11-16

These values are for lentils boiled without salt. Lentils cook from dry without soaking, which makes them one of the quickest legumes to prepare.
