Chickpeas, cooked: nutrition per 100 g

164 kcal, 8.9 g protein and 7.6 g fibre in every 100 grams.

These values are for chickpeas cooked from dry without salt. Canned chickpeas usually contain added salt, so their sodium figure will be higher.
