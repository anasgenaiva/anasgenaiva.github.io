    Whole-wheat pasta, cooked nutrition per 100 g

    149 kcal, 6 g protein and 3.9 g fibre in every 100 grams.

    Energy149kcal

    These values come from the USDA record “Pasta, whole-wheat, cooked (Includes foods for USDA's Food Distribution Program)” and are cooked, so they include the water the food absorbs. Other forms of whole-wheat pasta have their own USDA records with different numbers.

    149kcal / 100 g

    How whole-wheat pasta compares

    • Protein: 6 g per 100 g, 22nd of the 40 foods on this site.
    • Fibre: 3.9 g, 16th of 40.
    • Protein per 100 kcal: 4 g.
    • About 10% of its energy comes from fat and 16% from protein.
