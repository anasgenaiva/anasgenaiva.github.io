    Almonds nutrition per 100 g

    579 kcal, 21.2 g protein and 12.5 g fibre in every 100 grams.

    Energy579kcal

    These values are for plain almonds. Most of their energy comes from fat, which is why a small handful (about 28 g) already carries a lot of calories.
