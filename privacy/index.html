Privacy

Plate Notes has no accounts, forms that send data, or cookies of its own. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which keeps standard server logs.

Analytics is not switched on. If Google Analytics 4 is added later, this page will say so.

The portion calculator runs entirely in your browser; nothing you type is sent anywhere.

Plate Notes: per-100 g food facts from USDA FoodData Central, and a lab for learning Google Search Console. General information, not medical advice.
